FAQ

Questions, answered.

Production copy can be customized to match the client's actual booking policies.

How do I book a cab?+
Enter your pickup and destination, select the required vehicle and submit the booking request. A production implementation can connect this interface to a live backend and driver-dispatch system.
Do you provide airport transfers?+
Yes. The service can support advance airport pickup and drop reservations with scheduled dispatch and driver details.
Can I book an outstation taxi?+
One-way and round-trip outstation bookings can be supported. Final fare rules should be configured according to the operator's actual routes and policies.
Do you offer corporate transportation?+
Corporate plans can include employee transportation, visitor pickups, scheduled rides, monthly billing and dedicated support.
Which payment methods can be integrated?+
A production system can integrate UPI, cards, wallets and other supported gateway methods. The actual payment provider should be selected based on the business requirements.
